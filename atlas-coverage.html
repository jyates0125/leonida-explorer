How we reach real parity

Use licensed per-marker coordinates, proper game scope, accurate subtype, source URL, source license, location confidence, event availability and optional photo permission. The Atlas map automatically creates named filters from source-tagged records; empty filters stay hidden on the map rather than inventing marker positions. Coordinate-less reference entries remain in the catalog. GTA VI records are provisional until verified with gameplay.
