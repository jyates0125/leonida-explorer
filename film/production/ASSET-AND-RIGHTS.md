# Attribution and release-rights checklist

**Credits are recognition, not permission.** The words “community research” and “fan-made” describe provenance and uncertainty; they do not waive Rockstar/Take-Two rights, map tile licenses, or the consent of living people whose images/audio appear.

## Three evidence labels throughout the website

- **OFFICIAL / ROCKSTAR:** Statements and imagery that appear on a directly identified, official Rockstar Games or Take-Two source. Link to original. The official date was November 19, 2026 as reviewed November 13, 2026; re-check at deployment.
- **COMMUNITY R&D / ATTRIBUTED:** Community maps, interpretations, coordinate research, and datasets used under their own terms. Acknowledge the individual researchers by name when available (GTADB contributors, YANIS, DUPZOR, GTAMap.io/Mapheist where applicable), source URL, license, and version. Keep GTA VI geometry provisional until in-game verified.
- **FAN FICTION / UNVERIFIED:** Original storylines, fictional Cmac-Lupe-55th Street scenes, boat voyage to Leonida, speculative GTA VI placement, video graphics, invented dialogue, inferred spawn markers. Never present as Rockstar-approved facts or confirmed game content.

## Explicit contributions

- Thank **Rockstar Games** and **Take-Two Interactive** for creating and publishing GTA and its characters/worlds. All respective IP remains theirs.
- Thank **GTADB.org and all contributing mappers**, with links to data/code project. Respect MIT license for software and CC BY 4.0 for published community coordinates/tiles if applicable; license terms vary by asset and should be confirmed before distributing copied media.
- Thank **YANIS, DUPZOR, Mapheist and other individually attributed researchers** where their specific content is used. Credit names are not a blanket license for screenshots or tiles.
- Thank **GTAWeb.eu, GTA Wiki, GTA BOOM, PowerPyx** as research/usability references only where relevant; do not imply their databases were freely imported.
- Thank player contributors for **consented** gameplay captures and custom characters. Keep contributor handles only with approval.
