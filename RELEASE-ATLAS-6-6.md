# Atlas 6.6 — Dedicated Leonida Research Workspace

New `atlas-research.html`: a dedicated full-height Research interface inspired by the functional information architecture of GTADB.net, **not copied site code, photos or interface artwork**.

- Searches source records, name, descriptions, tags, regions, addresses and approximate world coordinates.
- Shows records without coordinates in results rather than inventing pins.
- Source-family toggles, seven main location groups, source details, optional source-provided photos, save visited/favorite places locally, share deep links and CSV export.
- Clickable pins, zoom, pan, focus, route sketch up to 12 stops and visible source provenance. No claim of verified driving directions.
- Sources `data/gtadb-6.json` generated by the licensed-source GitHub Action, with starter `gtadb-v6.json` fallback. Imports a user-authorized JSON snapshot into the current session. No GTADB.net proprietary bulk data is included.
- Removed duplicate Research basemap choice from Scaly/Loki selector; Research now stands alone as its own page.
- Existing GTA maps and Cayo Perico remain.

## Limits
Community tile and landmark content is CC BY 4.0 as credited to GTADB.org and contributors; distribution must comply with upstream terms, including credit. Actual tile availability and any alternate image licensing require checking before deployment. 2706 markers and 93 subcategories reported by GTADB.net are **not** claimed to be locally included unless a compatible authorized dataset is imported. GTADB.net source feed is not redistributed or scraped. Coordinates are provisional; source records without verified positions remain searchable without pins. Local browser interaction verification is still required.
