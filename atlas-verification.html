Verification rules: no inflated claims

LabelWhat the label guarantees
Source-checked countA public guide supports the size of the set, not each numbered slot location.
Reference indexAn entry in our manually curated index, with a category source link; price, stats, eligibility and coordinates are not individually verified.
Source-attributed landmarkCommunity mapping under GTADB attribution, not confirmation of an in-game reward, spawn or business.
