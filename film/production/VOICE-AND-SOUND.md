We aim for recognizable dramatic energy, **not voice cloning, unauthorized impersonation or imitating a specific actor's exact voice**. All final lines require original voice actors or properly authorized performances. GTA character dialogue can be original text spoken by a suitable performer, with transparent fan-fiction labeling.

**Cue palette:**
- 01 Homecoming — soft warm bass, sparse guitars or plucked synth, gentle city ambience, bus brakes / footsteps.
- 02 Paleto — accelerating original hero theme, percussion, engines, rubber skid, distant rotor blades, impacts. Avoid wall-to-wall booms.
- 03 City montage — alternate broad overhead silence and close-up Foley; pan radio dialogue to center so it's legible.
- 04 Yeti — hard comedic silence, single breath, distant bird, massive roar, then quick slapstick hits. “Everything will C alright” must be clear and sympathetically performed.
- 05 Harbor — full heroic motif, horns/strings/modern synth, harbor gulls, motorboats, two-handed rescue Foley.
- 06 Goodbye — piano and gentle strings, fireworks at moderate volume, no copyrighted soundtrack samples.
- 07 Impact — sub-bass anticipation, one huge metallic impact, water rush, underwater heartbeat, then intentional silence.
- 08 Leonida — airy coastal ambience and an unresolved two-note motif, faint radio static.

**Mix guidance**: Dialogue is highest priority. Side-chain music gently under speech. Provide closed captions and an audio-described variant if practical. Render final stereo at 48 kHz, peak ceiling around -1 dBTP, integrated target approximately -16 LUFS for web, and retain stems so sound effects can be mixed separately.
