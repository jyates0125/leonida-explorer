## Ground truth / boundaries
Collection counts have citations, not position-by-position testing. GTADB community landmarks do not imply gameplay spawns. GTA VI research is not final gameplay verification. GTA V is NOT claimed to be an exhaustive catalog of every vehicle, gun, collectible spawn, garage, activity, or mission. No third-party unlicensed proprietary databases are silently copied.
