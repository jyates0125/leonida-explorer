Verified facts and limitations

Atlas 6.8 is the development milestone, not a claim that the live main branch has been updated. GTA VI geography, road graphs and events are provisional. Imported landmarks are not automatically verified pickups, spawns or owned properties.

The current UV Blueprint and UV II options are stylized existing map imagery, not yet distinct illustrated UV source artwork. Source licensing and artwork testing are planned for Atlas 6.9.
