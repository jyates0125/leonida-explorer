About this explorer · Credits & permissions

Unofficial, free fan-made mapping project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. GTA names and game artwork belong to their respective rights holders. Community-reconstructed GTA VI geography is speculative and may differ from the released game. Research landmarks are not verified collectible locations.

Map sources: VIRUXE GTA V tiles (underlying game artwork © Rockstar Games; republication permission not established) · GTADB community research and Leonida reconstruction · CC BY 4.0 (subject to individual asset rights). Leaflet mapping library (BSD 2-Clause).

Support: GTA Map Explorer is free. No donations, paid features, advertising, or payment collection are enabled in this build. Any future support feature will be reviewed against applicable content permissions first.

Map imagery loads from external servers and requires an internet connection. If an external tile service is unavailable or changes its terms, imagery may fail to display. Save your personal notes and hunt data using the available export tools.
