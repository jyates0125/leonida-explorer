# ONE LAST RIDE — LOS SANTOS FOREVER
**Original fan-fiction screenplay treatment / Team Jura & Elario Studios**

**Genre:** friendship-driven action comedy with a dramatic cliffhanger. **Canon:** non-canonical fan tribute. **Estimated finished running time:** 7–10 minutes once performances and animated footage exist; **current finished media:** only the separately labeled 36-second motion preview.

## Prologue — The shock [00:00–00:20]
Night. Open water. The luxurious Aquarius glides toward darkness. Thunder, then an impossible metallic blow from underneath. Lights burst. Water enters. LOKI shouts: **“Scaly! Where are you?!”** A hand emerges through foam. We cut to black before answering. Super: **TWELVE HOURS EARLIER.**

## Scene 1 — Fifty Fifth [00:20–01:15]
Morning. A bus pulls to the curb outside the fictionalized neighborhood of 55th Street Alley in Los Santos. Cmac steps off, spotted instantly by LUPE, who runs forward and embraces him. No exposition is necessary: it is a warm, human beat. Lupe remains in this opening only. Cmac turns the corner, walking past the photographed-style alley frontage to Franklin's **fictional** neighborhood tree shop. FRANKLIN, LAMAR and CJ are trading jokes beside custom vehicles. Cmac sees them: **“ON FIFTY FIIIIIFTH STRRRREET! YA FIVE ME?!”** Their laughter gives the viewer one last ordinary day in Los Santos. Cut north to the roar of engines.

## Scene 2 — Two of a kind [01:15–02:30]
PALETO BAY. On forest roads beneath Mount Chiliad, LOKI (long scarlet hair, decorated red top hat and red suit) and SCALY MAN (unmasked green hair, shimmering purple-and-green galaxy outfit) drive separately. Ambush: an aggressive convoy closes from behind, hostile helicopter ahead. Loki weaves through a roadblock; Scaly is trapped on the far side of a fallen barrier. SFX: rotor chop, gravel, collapsing metal. Loki wheels around, refusing to leave. 

**SCALY (dry Midwestern calm):** “You're supposed to be getting outta here.”

**LOKI (quick, rough, playful):** “Aye, and who's gonna keep you out of trouble?”

Their cars push through, coordinated rather than invincible. Each narrowly saves the other. Their shared heroic motif begins as a small three-note hook and expands with orchestral drums.

## Scene 3 — Roads to the water [02:30–03:40]
Intercut the Paleto escape with city crews: MICHAEL rejoins in a sleek custom; TREVOR thunders over rough roads in his rusty **Bodhi**; IMANI sends updates from her tech station; MUTT and the Acid Lab create a distraction; PAVEL prepares transfers near the yacht; CJ, LAMAR and FRANKLIN lead the neighborhood convoy. Camera geography stays consistent: NORTH → CITY → WATERFRONT. The crowd is varied and inclusive without duplicating actors or mislabeling characters. Not every character requires dialogue; strong reaction shots are more effective.

**IMANI (radio):** “Keep moving. I've got a route to the water.”

**TREVOR:** “That better include MY truck!”

## Scene 4 — The treeline [03:40–04:30]
The last of the convoy approaches the wooded coastline. Cmac breaks away when he sees a silhouette in the trees: a towering YETI. Everyone freezes. He raises his hands, not a weapon.

**CMAC:** “ON FIFTY FIIIIIFTH STRRRREET! YA FIVE ME?!”

**CMAC (gentler):** “Everything will C alright. Don't commit suicide!”

The Yeti hesitates. Cmac smiles. It ROARS. His friends scramble to help; the scene becomes a slapstick scramble rather than a graphic injury. Smoke. Bright lights. Cmac is gone. We never show a body or explain the disappearance.

A half-second dream flash: Cmac's old phrase about needing **“some clue for blue”** echoes in Loki's memory. For one strange frame Loki and Scaly wear all-blue ceremonial suits. The beat instantly snaps back to the battle. Distinct from actual time continuity.

## Scene 5 — The harbor [04:30–05:50]
The pursuit converges at the waterfront. Cars stop on land. Characters transfer to speedboats or helicopters; none impossibly drives onto the yacht. Explosions illuminate the harbor without covering important character blocking. Scaly catches Loki as the last boat pulls away. Their hands lock; for the first time the music resolves to major-key brass.

**SCALY:** “I got you.”

**LOKI:** “Aye, you always do.”

They climb aboard the upgraded **Aquarius Galaxy Super Yacht**, exhausted and grinning. The last hostile aircraft recedes. Wide overhead: tiny humans safe beneath a huge amber sky.

## Scene 6 — Los Santos forever [05:50–06:55]
A calm transition. Crew gathered aboard the Aquarius. Franklin, Lamar, CJ, Michael, Trevor, Mutt, Imani, Pavel, Loki, Scaly and supporting business characters have their own brief moments. The tone is communal, not a roll call. The story temporarily yields to **player-provided clips** only when the contributor permitted their use. Warm original piano over waterfront reflections.

**LOKI:** “Think we'll ever come back?”

**SCALY:** “Man, Los Santos is gonna be right there.”

**Narration:** “Some came here for money. Some came looking for trouble. Somewhere along the way, we found our people.”

No answer about Cmac. A crackly radio transmits half a syllable. They glance at each other. Wind.

## Scene 7 — The impossible impact [06:55–07:40]
The yacht pushes into open water. A dull bass rumble grows beneath the water. An unidentified object strikes the hull. Metal shrieks. Lighting shifts from amber to moonlit blue. Water erupts across the deck; people are swept in different directions. Loki reaches through spray for Scaly. We glimpse crimson fabric, then purple and green, and a glimpse of someone swimming. **No bodies, no definitive endings.**

**LOKI:** “SCALY!”

**SCALY:** “SWIM, LOKI!”

The theme fragments into underwater heartbeat. Silence. Black. **ONE LAST RIDE.**

## Epilogue / GTA VI handoff [07:40–08:15]
A separate fan-fiction scene to introduce the sequel's *setting*—not an official claim that these characters actually meet. Dawn on a **Leonida Keys** beach. JASON and LUCIA discover a small object with the Team Jura insignia washed ashore. No explanation. Somewhere in VICE CITY, an unknown driver passes through neon light in a one-off glimpse. Cmac's line crackles over a handheld radio: **“Everything will C alright…”** Cut to black before identification. End card: **THE OCEAN KEPT ITS SECRETS.**
